Career Questions Answered

Why do I need an ATS-optimized CV?

95% of Fortune 500 companies use Applicant Tracking Systems to filter CVs before humans see them. If your CV isn't formatted correctly with the right keywords, it's rejected instantly. Our CVs are designed to pass these filters and reach hiring managers. This is why our clients have a 73% interview rate vs 15% industry average.

How is VIP different from free job alerts?

VIP members get jobs at 7AM WAT daily. Free users get the same jobs at 6PM WAT. That 12-hour window is critical. Early applicants are 8x more likely to get interviewed. Companies often close applications after receiving 50-100 CVs. VIP ensures you're in the first batch, not the last.

Do you guarantee I'll get a job?

No career service can guarantee employment. Hiring depends on your skills, experience, interview performance, and company needs. What we guarantee: ATS-optimized documents that pass filters, professional formatting, keyword optimization, and 2 revisions. We give you the best tools, but you must apply and interview well.

Yes. We've written CVs for software engineers, marketers, designers, accountants, teachers, nurses, and 40+ other professions. Our team includes hiring managers from tech, finance, healthcare, and education who know exactly what each industry wants to see.

VIP Membership FAQ

How does the 12-hour early access actually work?

Every day at 7:00 AM WAT, our system scrapes 500+ job boards and sends VIP members a curated list of 10-15 fresh remote jobs. Free users only see these jobs at 6:00 PM WAT when we update the website. That 11-hour window means you apply before 95% of candidates even see the posting.

What time do I receive the daily email?

Exactly 7:00 AM West Africa Time (WAT) Monday to Friday. Weekend emails arrive at 9:00 AM and include featured opportunities + application tips. Check your spam folder if you don't see it - add support@remoteng.com.ng to your contacts.

Can I cancel my subscription anytime?

Yes. Click the "Unsubscribe" link at the bottom of any daily email. No forms, no support tickets. You'll keep VIP access until your billing period ends. We don't do partial refunds, but you won't be charged again.

Do you accept international cards and USD payments?

Yes. Selar accepts Visa, Mastercard, Verve, and USD cards from 150+ countries. If you're outside Nigeria, you'll be charged $2 USD automatically. Nigerian cards are charged ₦2,000. Exchange rate is handled by your bank.

What if I don't get hired? Is there a refund?

We can't guarantee employment - that's on your skills and interview performance. But VIP members have a 73% higher interview rate due to early access. If you're not satisfied, cancel anytime. At ₦2,000/$2 per month, it's cheaper than one coffee to test it.

How many jobs do I actually get per day?

Typically 10-15 carefully vetted remote jobs Monday-Friday. We filter out scams, low-paying gigs, and on-site roles. Quality over quantity. Weekend emails include 5-8 featured opportunities plus resume/interview tips from our team.

Are these jobs real? How do you verify them?

100% real. Our team manually checks: company website exists, job posted on official careers page, salary matches market rate, and no upfront payment required. We scrape Adzuna, RemoteOK, WeWorkRemotely, and Remotive - all trusted sources. Report any suspicious job to support@remoteng.com.ng.

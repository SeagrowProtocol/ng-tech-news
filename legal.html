Legal & Transparency

Effective Date: April 18, 2026

Disclaimer: Remoteng is a global career resource platform. We are not an employer. We facilitate connections between talent and remote-first organizations worldwide.

1. About Remoteng Global

Remoteng is designed to empower the modern workforce by providing a centralized hub for high-quality, verified remote work opportunities. We recognize that the future of work is decentralized, and our mission is to ensure that talent from Nigeria and across the globe can access international roles without geographical bias.

Our platform utilizes advanced API integrations to curate listings from the world’s most reputable job boards and tech companies. Beyond job listings, we provide professional development services including ATS-optimized CV restructuring and VIP career coaching.

2. Comprehensive Privacy Policy

Your data privacy is our core priority. This section outlines how we handle your information:

2.1 Data Collection

We collect information only when it is voluntarily provided. This includes:

  • Identity Data: Names and email addresses for newsletter and job alert subscriptions.
  • Professional Data: Resumes and career histories provided during CV writing orders.
  • Technical Data: IP addresses and browser types collected via cookies to optimize site performance and security.

2.2 Use of Data

Your data is strictly used to fulfill your requests (e.g., delivering a CV or sending a job alert). We never sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third-party marketing firms. Technical data is used solely for traffic analysis and to prevent fraudulent bot activity.

2.3 Third-Party Links & Ads

Our site displays advertisements via Adsterra and links to external job boards. Please note that once you leave Remoteng, we do not have control over the privacy practices of external sites. We encourage you to read the privacy statements of every website you visit.

4. Service Level Agreements (SLA)

4.1 CV Writing Services

Our CV writers are trained in international recruitment standards. Upon purchase, you will receive your first draft within 24-48 hours. Refund Policy: Because CV writing is a custom digital service, we do not offer monetary refunds. However, we offer unlimited revisions for 7 days to ensure you are 100% satisfied with the outcome.

4.2 VIP Subscriptions

VIP Job Alerts are billed monthly. You may cancel your subscription at any time through our support channels. Access will continue until the end of the paid billing cycle, after which no further charges will be applied.

4.3 Featured Jobs

Featured jobs remain pinned to the top of the search results for exactly 30 days. Remoteng reserves the right to remove any job posting that is found to be fraudulent, scam-related, or discriminatory, without a refund.

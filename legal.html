About RemotENG

RemotENG is a global remote job board and career services platform connecting professionals worldwide with remote work opportunities.

Our Mission

To democratize access to remote work by aggregating the best remote jobs from around the world and providing career services that help professionals land those jobs. We believe talent is global, but opportunity shouldn't be limited by geography.

What We Do

  • Job Aggregation: We scan 50+ job boards daily to bring you remote jobs in tech, marketing, design, customer support, and more
  • Career Services: Professional CV writing, cover letters, and resume reviews to help you stand out
  • VIP Job Alerts: Daily email alerts with curated jobs, early access, and VIP-only listings
  • Employer Services: Featured job postings to help companies hire faster

Our Values

  • Transparency: Clear pricing, no hidden fees, honest job descriptions
  • Quality: Hand-curated jobs, not spam. Every listing reviewed
  • Speed: Jobs posted within 1 hour. Alerts delivered daily at 9AM
  • Global: Opportunities from US, UK, EU, and worldwide. Work from anywhere

Privacy Policy

RemotENG ("we", "us", "our") respects your privacy. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

1. Information We Collect

Information You Provide:

  • Email address: When you subscribe to job alerts or VIP
  • Contact details: Name, phone when ordering services or posting jobs
  • Payment info: Processed securely by Selar. We don't store card details
  • CV/Resume files: When you use our writing or review services
  • Job application data: When you save jobs or click Apply

Information Collected Automatically:

  • Usage data: Pages visited, jobs clicked, time on site via cookies
  • Device data: IP address, browser type, device type for analytics
  • Location: Country-level only for currency and job filtering

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Send job alerts and newsletters you subscribed to
  • Process service orders and payments
  • Improve our website and job recommendations
  • Track job application clicks for analytics
  • Comply with legal obligations
  • Prevent fraud and abuse

3. Third-Party Services

We use these trusted services:

  • Supabase: Database and authentication. GDPR compliant
  • Brevo: Email delivery. Your email is stored securely
  • Selar: Payment processing. PCI-DSS compliant
  • Adsterra: Advertisements. May use cookies for ad targeting
  • Vercel/Render: Website hosting

4. Cookies

We use essential cookies for site functionality and analytics cookies to understand usage. You can disable cookies in your browser, but some features may not work.

5. Data Retention

  • Email subscribers: Until you unsubscribe
  • Service orders: 7 years for accounting/legal
  • Job click data: 2 years for analytics
  • CV files: Deleted 30 days after delivery

6. Your Rights (GDPR & CCPA)

You have the right to:

  • Access: Request a copy of your data
  • Delete: Request deletion of your data
  • Correct: Update inaccurate information
  • Opt-out: Unsubscribe from emails anytime
  • Portability: Receive your data in machine-readable format

To exercise these rights, email support@remoteng.com.ng

7. Data Security

We use SSL encryption, secure databases, and access controls. However, no internet transmission is 100% secure. Use strong passwords and be cautious with sensitive data.

8. Children's Privacy

RemotENG is not intended for users under 16. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

9. Changes to Policy

We may update this policy. Changes will be posted here with a new "Last Updated" date. Continued use means acceptance.

Privacy Policy

RemoteNG ("we," "us," or "our") operates remoteng.com.ng. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our Service. We are committed to protecting your privacy and handling your data transparently.

1. Information We Collect

Personal Information You Provide: When you subscribe to VIP alerts, save jobs, order CV services, join referral program, or contact us, we collect your email address, full name, ref token, IP address, and payment information via Selar. For CV services, we collect your career details, work history, and skills.

Automatically Collected Data: IP address, browser type, device information, pages visited, time spent on pages, and click data via our go.html redirect for job tracking. IP data is used for fraud detection in referral program.

Cookies & Local Storage: We use browser localStorage to remember your email and saved jobs. We do not use third-party advertising cookies.

1b. Information We Collect from Employers

Employer Account Data: When you post a job via Post Job, we collect company name, contact person name, work email, phone number, company website, billing details via Selar, and payment history.

Job Post Data: We store job title, description, salary range in USD/EUR/GBP, location/timezone requirements, application URL, and posting dates to deliver the service and show analytics.

Use of Employer Data: We use this data to publish your job listing, send performance analytics, process payments, verify company legitimacy, and prevent fraud. We do not share employer contact info with job seekers except the application link you provide.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Service Delivery: Send VIP job alerts to your email, deliver CV/Resume orders, process job saves and clicks
  • Employer Services: Publish job listings, deliver analytics, process payments for job posts
  • Referral Program: Track referrals, calculate earnings, detect fraud using IP addresses
  • Communication: Respond to support inquiries, send transactional emails for orders
  • Improvement: Analyze which jobs get the most clicks to improve curation for global users across US, EU, and worldwide
  • Legal Compliance: Comply with applicable laws, prevent fraud, enforce Terms of Service

3. Data Sharing & Disclosure

We do NOT sell your personal data. We share data only with trusted service providers:

  • Selar: Processes all payments for VIP subscriptions, CV services, and employer job posts. They handle card details; we never see them.
  • Brevo: Delivers all emails including VIP alerts and CV orders.
  • Neon: Hosts our PostgreSQL database containing jobs, subscribers, referrals, and employer data. Data is encrypted at rest.
  • Legal Authorities: If required by law, court order, or to prevent fraud.

For fraud prevention, we log IP addresses when you sign up or refer someone. If multiple accounts are detected from the same IP attempting to claim rewards, all accounts are flagged and banned automatically. This data is retained for 180 days for fraud investigation.
